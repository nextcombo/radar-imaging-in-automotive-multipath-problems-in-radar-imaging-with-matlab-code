# Radar Imaging in Automotive: Multipath Problems in Radar Imaging [with MATLAB Code]

The front-view radar you are designing and simulating is a Uniform Linear Array (ULA) operating at 77 GHz, which is a workhorse in the W-band. For the DoA (Direction of Arrival), your client wants the maximum resolution (at the bottom of the array) to be ρθ = 3.6 degrees. The system must operate at 77 GHz (W-band), with a bandwidth B = 2 GHz, and it will transmit linearly modulated linear frequency-modulated signals. First, you will design a simple monostatic array based on these specifications and analyze and evaluate the impact of multipath propagation. You will confirm your assumptions by simulating your designed system.
After that, you need to design a bistatic array, where one antenna transmits while another receives backscatter. In this case, multipath effects must also be analyzed and confirmed through simulation. Finally, you must design a MIMO radar to reduce costs. In this scenario, you will need to determine the number of transmit/receive antennas, the number of antennas, and the appropriate distance between elements. Similarly, multipath effects must be evaluated through simulation.
